Stallin Conference

The Stallin Conference is one of Pakistan's most prominent ophthalmic gatherings, bringing together leading eye surgeons, clinical specialists, and medical suppliers under one roof. Ramay Electromedix has been a consistent major participant, showcasing the latest IOLs, diagnostic machines, and surgical instruments to the country's ophthalmic community.
