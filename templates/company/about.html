Our Story

Serving Pakistan Since 1996

Ramay Electromedix was founded with a single purpose: to make world-class ophthalmic technology accessible to every hospital and clinic across Pakistan. Starting with a small portfolio of intraocular lenses, we have grown into the country's most trusted authorized distributor of ophthalmic products.

Today we supply premium IOLs, surgical instruments, Japanese-manufactured diagnostic machines, and a comprehensive range of consumables to over 100 hospital clients spanning all four provinces.

Our team of biomedical engineers, clinical support specialists, and logistics professionals ensures that every product reaches its destination in perfect condition — and that every piece of equipment installed is fully supported through its operational life.
