Company Overview

Who We Are

Ramay Electromedix is Pakistan's most established authorized distributor of ophthalmic products, founded in 1996 in Lahore. Over 28 years, we have built an unmatched distribution network covering hospitals, eye clinics, and surgical centres across all four provinces.

We specialize in importing, registering, and distributing premium intraocular lenses, ophthalmic surgical instruments, Japanese diagnostic equipment, and ophthalmic consumables — handling every aspect of the supply chain from DRAP registration through to post-sales technical support.

Our client base includes government teaching hospitals, private eye hospitals, and specialist clinics. We are the preferred supply partner for some of Pakistan's most respected ophthalmic surgeons.
