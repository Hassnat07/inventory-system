Our Commitment

Quality at Every Step

Ramay Electromedix is committed to supplying only authentic, properly registered, and correctly stored ophthalmic products. We view quality assurance not as a regulatory obligation but as a patient safety imperative.

Every product we supply carries full manufacturer traceability, valid DRAP registration, and has been handled within its specified storage conditions from the time of manufacture to delivery at the surgical facility.

Sourcing

Products sourced exclusively from original manufacturers or their authorised distributors — no grey-market or repackaged items.

Storage

Climate-controlled warehouse maintaining temperature, humidity, and light conditions per manufacturer specifications.

Delivery

Cold-chain logistics for temperature-sensitive products. Each delivery includes product documentation and batch certificates.

Quality Concern or Complaint?

We take every quality concern seriously. If you observe any product defect, packaging damage, or cold-chain breach, please contact us immediately so we can investigate and take corrective action.
