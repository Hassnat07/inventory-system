DRAP Registration

All medical devices and ophthalmic products we distribute are registered with DRAP under the Medical Devices Rules 2017. Registration certificates are available on request.

Import Permits

We hold valid import permits for all regulated products and maintain complete documentation for each shipment in accordance with DRAP import requirements.

Lot Traceability

Full batch traceability is maintained from manufacturer to end-user. In the event of a product recall, we can trace all affected units to specific hospital clients within 24 hours.
